# Business Glossary

## Customer Analytics Terms

**Churn**: When a customer stops purchasing from the platform. Defined as no purchase activity for 90+ days.

**CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)**: The total revenue a business can expect from a single customer account throughout their relationship.

**RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)**: A segmentation model that ranks customers based on how recently they purchased, how often they purchase, and how much they spend.

**Recency**: Number of days since a customer's last purchase. Lower = more recent = better.

**Frequency**: Total number of purchases made by a customer within the analysis period.

**Monetary**: Total spend by a customer within the analysis period.

**Cohort**: A group of customers who made their first purchase in the same time period (e.g. same month).

**Cohort Retention**: The percentage of customers from a cohort who return to purchase in subsequent months.

**A/B Testing**: A controlled experiment comparing two variants (A and B) to determine which performs better on a target metric.

**Conversion Rate**: Percentage of users who complete a desired action (e.g. purchase) out of total users.

**AOV (Average Order Value)**: Total revenue divided by number of orders in a given period.

**Engagement Score**: A composite metric measuring customer interaction with the platform (clicks, sessions, emails opened).

**Customer Tier**: Segmentation of customers by value — typically Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze based on CLV or spend.

**Campaign ROI**: Revenue generated by a marketing campaign minus campaign cost, divided by campaign cost.

**Rolling Active Users**: Count of unique customers who made at least one purchase in the last N days (e.g. 30/60/90 day windows).

**AUC-ROC (Area Under the ROC Curve)**: A threshold-independent measure of a classifier's ability to distinguish between churned and active customers. A score of 1.0 is perfect; 0.5 is random. In this project, the primary evaluation metric for all churn models.

**SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)**: A method for explaining individual model predictions by attributing each feature's contribution to the output. Used in this project to identify the top drivers of churn risk per customer and validate that the model is using business-sensible signals.

**RFM Composite Score**: A weighted combination of Recency (0.35), Frequency (0.25), and Monetary (0.40) quintile scores, ranging from 1.0 to 5.0. Higher scores indicate more valuable, engaged customers. Weights sourced from e-commerce industry benchmarks and documented in docs/methodology.md.
